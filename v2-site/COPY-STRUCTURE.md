### 3. THE THREE OUTCOMES

**Purpose:** Qualification filter + the entire client promise. This is what every engagement produces.

**Section headline:**
Every engagement. Three outcomes. No exceptions.

**Outcome 1: Value Amplification**
Increase your value to your clients. Bigger deals, deeper retention, higher LTV — because your presale process demonstrates understanding your competitors can't match.

**Outcome 2: Time Compression**
Compress your time-to-value. What took weeks takes days. What took days takes hours. Capacity unlocked, sales cycles shortened, clients onboarded faster.

**Outcome 3: Demand Generation**
Produce an asset that generates pipeline on its own. Not a report that sits in a drawer — a running system that creates qualified inbound without additional headcount.

**Anchor line:**
If a potential engagement can't hit all three, it's not our engagement.

It's not.

Your best people still spend days building proposals from scratch. Tribal knowledge walks out the door every time someone leaves. Your presale process — the thing that wins or loses every deal — runs on gut feel, recycled decks, and heroic individual effort.

Meanwhile, your competitors are starting to figure out that AI isn't a feature to bolt on. It's infrastructure to build on.

**Kicker:**
The firms that build growth intelligence first will compound the advantage. The rest will wonder what happened.

### 5. HOW IT WORKS — GROWTH ENGINEERING

**Purpose:** Show the engagement arc. High-level, 3 beats. The client sees a journey, not a service menu.

**Section headline:**
Growth Engineering: your initiative, our engine.

**Beat 1: Intelligence Discovery (Weeks 1-3)**
You define the growth initiative. We run targeted diagnostics — not a generic audit, but a specific investigation of the intelligence you're missing to get where you said you want to go. Output: a shared growth thesis and the architecture for the system we'll build.

**Beat 2: System Build (Weeks 4-8)**
We compose the system from modular intelligence capabilities — tuned to your methodology, your clients' language, your data. You're in the build with us weekly, shaping what gets built. This isn't a black-box handoff. It's co-creation.

**Beat 3: Activation & Compound (Weeks 9-12)**
System goes live. We measure against the three outcomes. The system starts compounding — generating leads, improving proposals, building your competitive moat. And because it's extensible, the question shifts from "is it done?" to "what do we add next?"

### 6. WHY NOT ENTERPRISE / WHY US

**Purpose:** Kill the "why not just buy Salesforce Einstein" objection.

**Section headline:**
This isn't enterprise software. And it isn't consulting.

**Column 1 — Enterprise platforms:**
Give you their data model. Their dashboards. Their view of your business. You bend your process to fit their assumptions. And when you need something custom, you're in a queue behind 10,000 other customers.

**Column 2 — Traditional consulting:**
Gives you a deck and a recommendations list. Smart people in a room for 6 weeks. Then they leave, and you're on your own to implement.

**Column 3 — Growth Intelligence:**
Gives you a running system built around your specific business — your methodology, your clients, your data. Engineered by someone who understands both the technology and your growth challenge deeply enough to build something that actually works. And it compounds.

### 7. PREREQUISITES

**Purpose:** Qualify the buyer and demonstrate that you're not selling magic — you're selling outcomes that require a real foundation.

**Section headline:**
Growth Intelligence works when the foundation is right.

**Copy:**
We don't pretend AI solves everything. Agentic systems produce real outcomes when three things are in place:

**A clear growth goal** — without it, the system automates motion, not progress.
**A GTM strategy** — without it, the system doesn't know who to target or what to say.
**A data governance plan** — without it, the system can't trust its own inputs.

If you have these, we'll build something that compounds. If you don't, we'll help you establish them before we build. Either way, the foundation comes first.

## Copy Principles (v2)

1. **Lead with the client's ambition, not our methodology** — they define the destination
2. **Concrete over abstract** — scrolling deliverables, specific outcomes, real numbers
3. **Engineer, not consultant** — language of building, systems, precision
4. **Composable and extensible** — these words do real work against enterprise competitors
5. **The system compounds** — the flywheel is the differentiator, reinforce it everywhere
6. **Qualify openly** — stating prerequisites builds trust and filters bad-fit prospects
7. **The assessment is a reference implementation** — never position it as the product
