The Operating Model

Each client gets a managed agency — bounded, governed, and continuously improving.

A Client Managed Agency is an isolated agentic system provisioned by ChesedClaw™, our headless orchestration harness, with defined access points, composable tools, and clear managed boundaries. Enterprise cloud or dedicated hardware. No external dependencies. We stay in the work.
