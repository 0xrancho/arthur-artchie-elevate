An audit you can walk.

The limit

An audit does not fix anything.

It tells you what is true and what to do about it, in order. It does not do it. That is the whole engagement, and we would rather say so now than have you find out in week three.

Plenty of firms take the document and do the work themselves. That is a good outcome and we will say so in the readout. Some ask us to run what we found; that is a separate conversation and it starts from the document. If you already know what is wrong and need someone to do it, an audit is the wrong instrument. We will tell you that on the call.
