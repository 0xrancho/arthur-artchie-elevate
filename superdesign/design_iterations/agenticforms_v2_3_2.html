The Problem

“A professional services firm’s growth, margins, and capacity are all constrained by the same thing: senior humans synthesizing complex client inputs and applying them to proprietary methodology. Until very recently, encoding that process into a technical system was impossible. That assumption is no longer true.”

— Joel Austin, Founder

The Shift

Give Your Client a 1-1 CTA.

Scaling client knowledge no longer depends on adding consulting hours. Give your clients experiences that externalize your methodologies, collect valuable data and context, and rapidly deliver formatted gaps, perspectives, inspiration, or the next human touchpoint. All invoked with your custom intuitive logic.

Your senior consultant gets promoted from note taker and slide formatter to context orchestrator.

This isn’t a fancy UI. An agentic client experience has three compounding outcomes:

+

Close Rate

An agentic form scales client knowledge across the firm. Context becomes interoperable across multiple deals and buyers within the same account. More context means better positioning and higher close rates.

−

Time-to-Value

Agentic forms let users self-segment, qualify, educate, and answer questions they didn’t know to ask. This collapses the number of human-to-human turns and the latency between them. The sales cycle collapses with it.

+

Demand Gen Asset

An agentic form is just as much an internal tool and client asset as it is a top-of-funnel lead magnet. Your methodology, available to an agent, positioned wherever you need it.

Process

How We Build It

01
Discovery

We learn where your methods and experience create the most value and where synthesis is costing you the most hours. We map the deterministic logic your senior people repeat on every engagement: The frameworks, the sequencing, the scoring, the decision trees that always resolve the same way. We also map where that logic stops at creative forks so the human can pick it up with full context and minimal overhead. This is what we encode.

02
Design

We architect a unique experience and the delivery format together. Every engagement is different. Some clients need a public-facing assessment embedded on their site. Others need an invite-only landing page for strategic partners. Others need an internal document generator that structures outputs for their delivery team. We scope to the highest-impact format for where you are, where you’re headed, and what will get you there fastest.

03
Build

The deliverable is an agentic form with custom logic, tools, and integrations. It’s a 1 of 1 experience for your clients and your consultants scoped to your specific use cases. We deploy wherever you need the solution, based on your data and governance requirements. This could be an iframe embedded on your website, a gated landing page with authenticated access, a full-stack application with connectors into your applications, or an agent deployed directly inside your managed environment.

04
Deploy

We launch fast and optimize live. Our focus is rapid delivery into rapid growth. Upon launch the form will start engaging users, collecting data, generating insights, and accelerating your deal flow. We measure, iterate, and expand scope as the system proves itself. Every cycle gets smarter.
