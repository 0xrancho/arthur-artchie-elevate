# Arthur & Archie Brand Guidelines

## Brand Overview

**Arthur & Archie** is a Growth Intelligence platform for trust-based professional services firms. We measure relationship trust across client portfolios to reveal hidden revenue opportunities, vulnerable relationships, and high-potential prospects.

---

## Brand Essence

### Mission
To bring analytical rigor to the art of professional relationships—transforming gut-feel business development into data-driven growth intelligence.

### Vision
Every professional services firm equipped with the trust intelligence to grow predictably and protect relationships proactively.

### Heritage
Seven generations of professional service excellence spanning 200+ years—from lending law books to Abraham Lincoln to pioneering AI-powered opportunity intelligence.

### Primary Tagline

### Secondary Positioning

## Brand Personality

### Voice Attributes

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Authoritative** | We speak from 200+ years of professional services expertise |
| **Analytical** | Data-driven, precise, methodical |
| **Warm** | Approachable despite sophistication; we're partners, not vendors |
| **Confident** | Bold claims backed by guarantees |
| **Direct** | No fluff; respect the reader's time |

### Tone Examples

**Do:** "Your top 20 accounts hold $2.3M in untapped revenue. We'll show you exactly where."

**Don't:** "We might be able to help you potentially find some opportunities in your client base."

### Writing Style
- Active voice
- Short sentences
- Specific numbers over vague claims
- Questions that provoke reflection
- Analogies that clarify complex concepts (e.g., "sabermetrics for professional services")

## Content Guidelines

### Headlines
- Lead with outcomes, not features
- Use numbers when possible
- Ask provocative questions

**Examples:**
- "Your clients trust you more than you realize. We measure it."
- "$2.3M in revenue is hiding in your top 20 accounts."
- "What if you could see relationship risk before clients leave?"

### Body Copy
- One idea per paragraph
- Max 3-4 sentences per paragraph
- Use subheads to break up long sections
- Include specific examples and data

### CTAs
- Action-oriented verbs
- Clear value proposition
- Urgency without desperation

**Examples:**
- "See Your Trust Score" (primary)
- "Get Your Growth Report" (primary)
- "Book a Strategy Call" (secondary)
- "Download the Guide" (tertiary)

## Brand Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Lead with trust as the differentiator
- Use specific numbers and guarantees
- Reference heritage authentically
- Maintain warm professionalism
- Show the methodology (SEA)

### Don't
- Use jargon without explanation
- Make claims without proof points
- Appear cold or overly corporate
- Ignore the human element of relationships
- Oversimplify complex client situations
